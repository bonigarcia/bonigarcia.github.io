Boni García
===========

I am an Associate Professor (with tenure) at <a href="http://www.it.uc3m.es/bogarcia/index.html">Universidad Carlos III de Madrid</a> in Spain. Most of my experience lies in software engineering with a special focus on test automation and, more recently, AI-assisted software development.

I am a <a href="https://www.selenium.dev/project/structure/#tlc">tech lead</a> at <a href="https://www.selenium.dev/">Selenium</a>, the creator and maintainer of <a href="https://github.com/bonigarcia/webdrivermanager">WebDriverManager</a>, and the lead developer of <a href="https://www.selenium.dev/documentation/selenium_manager/">Selenium Manager</a> (see all my open source projects on <a href="https://github.com/bonigarcia">GitHub</a>).

I wrote the books <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Mastering-Software-Testing-JUnit-Comprehensive-ebook/dp/B076ZQCK5Q/">Mastering Software Testing with JUnit 5</a> (Packt Publishing, 2017), <a href="https://oreil.ly/1E7CX">Hands-On Selenium WebDriver with Java</a> (O'Reilly Media, 2022), and <a href="https://hubs.la/Q04ksQ6g0">Context Engineering</a> (Manning, 2026). Also, I am the author of more than 60 research papers in different journals, magazines, and conferences.

I publish as open content all the material I create to teach at the university as <a href="https://bonigarcia.dev/learning-resources.html">learning resources for developers</a>. I am an international speaker at academic and professional conferences (<a href="https://bonigarcia.dev/publications.html">slides here</a>).
